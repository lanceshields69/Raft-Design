// Design maturity gap

# The design gap usually shows up before the design hire does.

At Series A, the product and team start growing faster than the decisions holding them together. You may already be seeing the signs.

✕ The product still needs someone to explain it. It works in a founder-led demo, but new users need help understanding the value or completing important workflows.

✕ Every release adds another version of the same pattern. With AI you ship features quickly, but the interface becomes less consistent and harder to extend.

✕ Design decisions are scattered across the team. Founders, PMs, and engineers make customer-experience decisions one ticket at a time.

✕ The company tells a different story in every place. The website, sales deck, demo, and product no longer feel like one company.

✕ You need senior judgment before you need a design department. The gap is already affecting the roadmap, but a full design team is still premature.

# Raft turns scattered decisions into a foundation the whole team can build from.

✓ Clear brand One story across product, sales, and marketing.

✓ Stronger core UX Important workflows that make sense without a guided demo.

✓ Reusable product system Shared patterns the team can extend without starting over.

# Brand first. Then the product that expresses it.

Why four weeks, not three: most sprints pick one lane. This does both, in the order that actually works.

01Define— Week 1

Direction-setting. Positioning, audience, and the design foundation everything else builds on.

02Brand— Week 2

Identity system: typography, color architecture, voice, and an investor-ready deck template.

03Product— Week 3

5–10 high-fidelity screens, interactive prototype, edge cases mapped, built on the Week 2 system.

04Systemize— Week 4

Tokenized component library, fully documented handoff, recorded engineering walkthrough. Your team builds on it without you in the room.

──[ 08 ]── FAQ ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

How is this different from hiring a freelancer?
You're working directly with someone who's led design at Adobe and Walmart scale, not delegating to a junior contractor. The output is also a full system, brand and product together, not a one-off deliverable.
What if we need more than 10 product screens?
The Full System Sprint covers most funded startups' core surfaces. Larger scopes are quoted directly after the fit assessment call.
Do you work with teams outside the US?
Raft works in English and Japanese.
What happens after the sprint ends?
Most teams either take what's delivered and run with it in-house, or move into the Land & Expand fractional retainer to keep shipping with continued design leadership.
How soon can we start?
Sprints typically start within four weeks of booking a fit assessment call, depending on current availability. Book a call to find the next open slot.
Will our product and roadmap details stay confidential?
Yes. Happy to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion, just ask.
Why can't I just use AI tools myself?
AI tools are powerful, but they don't know which choices are right for your specific business. The value isn't the AI, it's 25+ years of judgment directing it: knowing what to build, when it's right, and how to get there fast. Without that direction, AI accelerates guesswork just as easily as it accelerates good decisions.
How is this different from AI-Ready Design Systems?
The Sprint is a fixed, one-time engagement that builds your brand and product foundation from the ground up. AI-Ready Design Systems is for teams that already have a system in place and want it restructured so their team and their AI tools can work from it directly. Most funded startups start with the Sprint.

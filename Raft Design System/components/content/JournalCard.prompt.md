Article card. `index` is the two-up grid on /journal/; `carousel` is the bordered homepage card.

```jsx
<JournalCard image="assets/article-img-how-i-use-ai.webp" title="How I Use AI" excerpt="…" href="…" />
<JournalCard variant="carousel" image="assets/article-img-the-contradiction.webp" title="Why I Built a Raft, Not a Ship" href="…" />
```

Articles link out to Substack — the label is "Read on Substack" on the index and "→ Read" in the carousel.

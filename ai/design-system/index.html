# The way design systems work is changing.

Teams are already using AI tools, but most design systems weren’t built for them. The result is fast but inconsistent output, off-brand materials, and duplicated effort.

of organizations use AI in at least one business function

Reusable components and brand guidance give AI tools a stronger starting point.

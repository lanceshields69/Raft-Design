Opens a page section — brand-green pill, uppercase, 13px/900, 0.52px tracking.

```jsx
<SectionLabel>Approach</SectionLabel>
```

On the homepage it carries a large bottom margin (133px) before the section body. Most of these pills stay in English even on Japanese pages — the word is being used as a design element. Use `Eyebrow` instead for flat labels with no pill.

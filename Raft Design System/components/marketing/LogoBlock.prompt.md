The brand square. Never re-tint it: the fill is always `--lime`, RAFT is always `--dark` and DESIGN always `--white`, in both themes.

```jsx
<LogoBlock size={300} />
```

For nav and small placements use the animated marks instead: `assets/r-mark-dark.gif` (dark) / `assets/r-mark-light.gif` (light), or the static `assets/r-mark-logo.svg`.

## Offerings

### Design Foundation Sprint

Tagline: Your product outgrew your brand. Fix both in four weeks.

A four-week, fixed-price engagement for funded startups. It covers brand first, then the product that expresses it. Most sprints pick one lane, and this one does both, in that order.

Timeline:

1. Week 1, Define: positioning, audience, and the design foundation.
2. Week 2, Brand: identity system (typography, color architecture, voice) and an investor-ready deck template.
3. Week 3, Product: 5 to 10 high-fidelity screens, an interactive prototype, and mapped edge cases, built on the Week 2 system.
4. Week 4, Systemize: a tokenized component library, documented handoff, and a recorded engineering walkthrough.

Pricing, with 50% due to start and 50% at delivery:

- Core, $28,000. For teams with some existing design foundation. Includes positioning, audience, and experience principles; a core brand identity system; key sales and marketing templates; up to 5 core product screens in high fidelity; an interactive prototype; core states and edge cases; a component library and design tokens; documentation and engineering handoff; and an AI-ready design system (DESIGN.md). Everything is built and delivered in Figma.
- Full System, $34,000. For teams starting closer to zero. Includes everything in Core, plus an expanded brand system (icon and illustration style, deck, social, and email templates), a full messaging framework, 8 to 10 high-fidelity product screens, deeper edge-case mapping, and an expanded component library.
- Land & Expand, $10,000 per month. Ongoing fractional design leadership, roughly 15 hours a week, for teams that want to keep brand and product work running after the sprint without a full-time hire.

Raft is currently taking a limited number of sprint engagements in exchange for case-study rights. Sprints typically start within four weeks of booking a fit assessment call. A scoping note and timeline come back within 48 hours of a brief. An NDA can be signed before detailed discussion.

### AI-Ready Design Systems

Tagline: Build a design system your team and AI can work from.

Raft builds brand and product systems that give a team and its AI tools the same source of truth. A system includes brand foundations (strategy, positioning, visual identity), voice and content, design tokens, a component library, templates (decks, marketing, proposals, pages), a DESIGN.md file with structured context for AI tools, AI workflow setup and usage guidance, and implementation support such as handoff documents.

It is for teams that use AI tools and want consistent, on-brand output across marketing, sales, product and design, and engineering. Raft is tool-agnostic. One current workflow loads the full design system, brand rules, and DESIGN.md into Claude Design as context. The page also lists ChatGPT, Figma Make, Cursor, Lovable, and Canva AI.

Pricing and timeline are not published. Every Design Foundation Sprint includes an AI-ready design system. Raft's own brand system is built the same way and is published as the [Raft Design & Brand System deck](https://raftdesign.studio/design-system-deck/) (English only).

## Approach

We work on brand, product, and strategy together, because a strong brand cannot make up for a confusing product. We prototype early and stay with the team through design and build. AI is part of how the work gets made. Strategy forms in conversation with language models such as Claude and ChatGPT before any design tool opens. Design and prototype move together in Figma and AI tools, close enough to the real thing to test and share without waiting for engineering. Claude Code, Cursor, and V0 then turn the design into working software. The build is directed, not delegated, and people make the final calls. Raft publishes the AI tools it uses, with verdicts and a list of tools it dropped, at https://raftdesign.studio/tools/.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost?

The Design Foundation Sprint is $28,000 for Core and $34,000 for Full System, billed 50% to start and 50% at delivery. There is no hourly billing. Land & Expand fractional design leadership is $10,000 per month for roughly 15 hours a week. Pricing for other project work is not published, so ask on a call.

### How long does it take?

The sprint runs four weeks. Sprints typically start within four weeks of booking a fit assessment call, depending on availability. Land & Expand has no fixed end date.

### Should we fix the brand or the product first?

Brand first, then product, in the same sprint. Week 1 sets direction. Week 2 builds the brand system. Week 3 designs the product screens on that system. Week 4 turns it into a documented component library.

### Does Raft only design, or also build?

Both. The sprint delivers high-fidelity screens, an interactive prototype, and engineering handoff. On project work, Raft takes designs through to working software using Claude Code, Cursor, and V0. For example, the AI Design LA website was designed and built with Claude Code and then converted to WordPress.

### What do we have at the end of a sprint?

Everything is built and delivered in Figma. The Core deliverables include a brand identity system, templates, core product screens, an interactive prototype, a component library with design tokens, documentation, and a DESIGN.md file. The sprint also includes a recorded engineering walkthrough so the team can build without Raft in the room. The site does not state contract or ownership terms, so confirm them on the fit assessment call.

### How do AI tools use the design system?

The system includes a DESIGN.md file with structured context for AI tools, plus brand rules, voice guidance, design tokens, components, and templates. A team loads these into tools such as Claude Design, ChatGPT, Figma Make, Cursor, or Lovable so the output stays on brand. Raft is tool-agnostic.

### Will our product details stay confidential?

Yes. Raft will sign an NDA before any detailed discussion if asked.
